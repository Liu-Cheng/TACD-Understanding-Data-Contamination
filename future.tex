\section{Future Work}

Several aspects of this work warrant further investigation.

First, although our detector effectively identifies contaminated samples within benchmarks, the question of how to reliably evaluate model performance using a partially contaminated benchmark remains unaddressed. Future work may explore mitigation strategies that enable fair and accurate assessment despite the presence of a certain proportion of contaminated samples.

Second, the current detection framework primarily targets exact and near-exact copies of benchmark samples in training data. However, contamination may also take more subtle forms, for instance, when the same functionality is implemented with different variable names, reordered statements, or alternative control-flow structures. Extending the detection framework to cover such semantically equivalent yet syntactically diverse variants is a direction that warrants further exploration.
